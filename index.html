Options
Scores and tricky notes are saved on this device only.

Scale

No sound? Turn the iPhone's silent switch off and raise the volume. Headphones let you hear the real low register.

Faint low notes? Phone speakers can't reproduce the bottom of the bass. Turn on "Play an octave higher" in Settings.

Sound: real double bass pizzicato, recorded note by note on each string by the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.

How the board is drawn: you are looking down at your own bass, nut at the top, E string on the left. Light bands mark your home hand position for the chosen scale.
